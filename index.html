      Moolank · मूलांक

      Your birth day reduced to one digit. Born on the 23rd: 2 + 3 = 5. It describes your nature.

      Bhagyank · भाग्यांक

      All digits of the full birth date added and reduced. It describes your destiny.

      Namank · नामांक

      Letters carry Chaldean values (A=1, B=2, C=3, D=4, E=5, F=8 …). The total is reduced. Names are tuned so this is a friend of the Moolank.

      Vedic grid · अंक चक्र

      A 3×3 chart laid out 3‑1‑9 / 6‑7‑5 / 2‑8‑4. Zeros are dropped. Missing numbers show qualities to develop.
