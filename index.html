      Gyaan ज्ञान

      The nine numbers, their grahas, and how Ank Jyotish works.

      Moolank · मूलांक

      Your birth day reduced to one digit. Born on the 23rd: 2 + 3 = 5. It describes your nature and how others see you.

      Bhagyank · भाग्यांक

      All digits of your full birth date added and reduced. It describes your destiny and the path life pushes you toward.

      Namank · नामांक

      Each letter of your name has a value (Chaldean system: A=1, B=2, C=3, D=4, E=5, F=8, G=3, H=5 …). The total is reduced to one digit. Names are often adjusted so this number is a friend of the Moolank and Bhagyank.

      Vedic grid · अंक चक्र

      A 3×3 chart laid out 3‑1‑9 / 6‑7‑5 / 2‑8‑4, one box per graha. Zeros are dropped. Missing numbers show qualities to develop, usually through that graha's remedy.

      A note on belief

      Ank Jyotish is a traditional practice. Use it for reflection, not for medical, financial or legal decisions. Graha friendships and remedies vary between teachers; this app follows the Parashari natural friendship table.
